Thank You

Acknowledgments#

LemmaLoom was diesigned and built by Ryan S. Cooper, and special thanks go to Mary Lynne Gasaway Hill, PhD, FRSA for her guidance, feedback, and support of this project.

LemmaLoom also contains software, models, language data, and typefaces that other people made and shared under open licenses. A model here is a large set of numbers learned from many example recordings or texts. LemmaLoom uses its models to transcribe speech, tell speakers apart, and analyze grammar.

The credits below are the same as those under Licenses in LemmaLoom ▸ About LemmaLoom. Click an entry there to read the full text of its license or licenses (see Licenses). In that list, the Whisper models and their tokenizer files are credited under WhisperKit, the pyannote models under FluidAudio, and TypeScript, DOMPurify, and marked under Monaco Editor.
